# Revision and verification record — v1.1

Revised October 29, 2026. This release changes presentation and adds descriptive fields; it does not claim a new collection or independent recoding. Original v1 statuses, excerpts, sources and collection window remain intact. Git history preserves the original files.

## What changed

The question now concerns disclosures in a bounded review, rather than what a guest can discover. Access coverage is separate from disclosure. Negative results are labeled “not found in reviewed text,” not verified non-disclosure. Counts and dates are generated from study data. Mobile readers can use stacked hotel records. Methods, source access and correction history remain available.

## Remaining verification work

- Verify current operation and City of Milwaukee eligibility for every candidate, including the five pilot properties. Record the authoritative address/boundary evidence and date. Postal city alone is insufficient.
- Document how the 30-name frame was assembled and why candidates were included. Thirty is a planning target, not a statistical sample-size justification. The alphabetical pilot is a convenience for reproducibility, not representative sampling.
- Recollect the five properties with protocol v2 below. Retain v1 as a historical release. Do not overwrite the original collection window.
- Have a second reviewer independently code frozen evidence without seeing v1 decisions, then independently repeat discovery for a subset. Keep disagreements before adjudication. AI self-review does not supply independent reliability.
- Test guest comprehension before interpreting specificity as usefulness. Actual room conditions, sleep and commercial effects require separately designed studies.

## Source spot-checks during the audit

On October 29, 2026, web-extracted official pages again contained the three quoted positive examples: Brewhouse FAQ timed quiet hours; Ambassador amenities linen adjective; Best Western room-page quiet/restful wording. Aloft extraction still omitted relevant room detail. These checks corroborate quotations, not all negative codes, guest browser access or room conditions. ## Scope of assurance

Automated checks establish internal consistency and local route integrity. They cannot establish source completeness, hotel claim truth, research validity or WCAG conformance. No independent second coder, new participant research or municipal verification has been completed in this release.

## Corrections and citation

Report a correction through the repository Issues page with observation ID, source URL, relevant section and observation date. Preserve old and revised values and explain each change. Cite dataset version and collection date, not only the live URL. Original hotel text remains the property of its rights holders; repository publication does not grant rights to reuse that content.

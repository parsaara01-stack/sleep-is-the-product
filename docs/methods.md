# What this pilot can tell us

Sleep Is the Product asks whether official hotel pages tell guests something concrete about the conditions of a night’s stay. Parsa Ara’s first milestone is a five-property website pilot in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, reviewed October 29, 2026. It contains 25 property-domain observations and 13 official hotel URL records, including failed URLs. It is not a guest sleep study.

## Who was included

A fixed, purposive panel of 30 named candidates targets City of Milwaukee hotels. The first five alphabetically named entries were chosen for the pilot before coding: Aloft, Ambassador, Best Western Plus Airport, Brewhouse and Cambria. This is a transparent selection rule, not a random sample. Five official-address listings were located, but Cambria’s address was available only in indexed official text. Postal city labels do not independently establish municipal boundaries. Municipal eligibility remains to be checked before the panel expands; the other 25 are candidates, not completed observations.

The protocol and candidate list were committed before the coded table was created. Some literature and URL discovery preceded that commit. This is a protocol-led pilot, not a preregistered study. No hotel was swapped out because its site was difficult to access.

## What was read

The bounded review used official overview, room/accommodation and amenities/FAQ pages, up to four unique URLs per property. A targeted official-domain search helped discover room pages. The web extractor may serve cached page text. Search snippets were discovery leads; Cambria’s snippets were not coded because direct page access failed. Room photos, reviews, OTAs, prices and availability were excluded. Best Western’s extract carried default/cached booking dates; no dates were entered and no booking action was taken.

Short, source-linked excerpts are kept in the data. A source record describes access depth and a section locator; it is a curated research note, not an archived facsimile of the entire page. Checksums verify these saved notes and the reproducible data outputs, not the truth of a hotel claim or the continuing state of its website. The retrieval interval is recorded at session-window precision because the initial web tool calls did not retain exact timestamps. The access log distinguishes failed direct reads from indexed text.

## How a cell was coded

Five domains cover noise, light control, temperature control, bedding and sleep-specific guest choice. Prior hotel studies motivate the environmental categories. Guest choice is an investigator-defined information category, not a validated clinical scale.

Specific means a concrete feature or policy. Vague means relevant wording without a concrete specification. Not stated means no qualifying statement in the accessible reviewed page set. Not accessible means relevant pages or detail panels were unavailable and no positive statement could establish a code. Missing web information never means an amenity is absent.

The rules intentionally exclude bed size from bedding specificity and generic king/queen inventories from guest choice. A thermostat control would qualify; air conditioning alone would establish only a system claim. A bathroom mirror light alone would not establish guest control of bedroom light. Quiet hours qualify as a specific policy without proving a quiet room. An explicit statement of absence could count as specific information, but no absence was inferred here.

## What was observed

Among the 25 cells: one specific, two vague, 12 not stated and ten not accessible. The only specific claim under this rubric is Brewhouse’s stated 10 p.m.–6 a.m. quiet-hours policy. Ambassador’s linen wording and Best Western’s quiet/restful room wording are vague under the fixed definitions. Aloft’s incomplete room detail and Cambria’s inaccessible official pages account for the ten inaccessible cells. These are properties of the review process and available text, not service ratings.

There is no composite score, hotel ranking, statistical significance test or citywide prevalence estimate. In this nonrandom pilot, a proportion of five hotels would imply more generality than the design warrants. The website shows category counts and source evidence with the denominator visible.

## Sensitivity and next steps

If all quality adjectives about bedding were counted as specific, Ambassador’s bedding cell would change from vague to specific; the specific count would become two rather than one. That alternative is not the primary analysis because it answers a looser question. Search snippets could change some Cambria cells but remain excluded by the access rule. A different retrieval route or live browser review could change Aloft’s cells; these are not stable negative findings.

There is one AI-assisted initial coder and no independent reliability estimate. Before interpreting disclosure differences, a second coder should classify all 25 cells, reconcile disagreements and retry unavailable panels using the same bounded scope. Then verify geography and operating status, amend any ineligible candidates transparently, and collect the 30-property panel within a compact review window. Future partner validation could compare statements with room conditions and test guest comprehension. Actual sleep, willingness to pay, purchases and revenue require separate studies; none was measured, modeled or claimed here. No hotel or participant has been contacted.

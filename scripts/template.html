Methods and limitations

A bounded disclosure audit

Selection

The first {{PROPERTY_COUNT}} alphabetically named entries in a fixed purposive candidate panel were reviewed. The panel is not representative; municipal eligibility and current operation remain unverified. The larger panel is a planning target, not a sample-size justification.

Collection

Up to four official URLs per property; {{SOURCE_COUNT}} URL records include failed attempts. Extracted text may be cached. Dynamic panels were incompletely exposed. Short source notes cannot reconstruct the full negative search.

Coding

Original protocol v1 preceded coding but followed initial discovery; it was not preregistered. One AI-assisted initial coder; no independent reliability estimate. Specificity, access coverage, claim type and actionability are distinct.

Interpretation

Quiet hours describe policy, not sound levels. Air conditioning does not prove individual control; adjustable lighting does not prove daylight exclusion. Brand and material claims do not establish guest usefulness. No composite score or citywide inference.

Selected background literature

Why these domains?

These observational hotel studies motivate environmental questions. They do not validate the disclosure rubric or establish which information guests find useful. Guest requests are an investigator-defined category. This is selected background, not a systematic review.
